☀️ 破免护符 Sunder Charms

六系破免大型护符（火/冰/电/毒/物理/魔法），打破怪物免疫，让更多流派能 farm 全图。3.3 补丁起获取门槛提高：潜伏破免最低掉落等级 69→75，靠 魔法寻获 Magic Find 刷取掉率下调且仅限地狱难度。 Six Grand Charms (火焰 Fire/冰冷 Cold/闪电 Lightning/Poison/Physical/Magic) that break monster immunities, letting far more builds farm every zone. Since patch 3.3 they are harder to get: the Latent 破免 Sunder minimum drop level went 69→75, and 魔法寻获 Magic Find drops were reduced and are now Hell-only.

🌑 恐怖地带 Terror Zones

击败对应难度 巴尔 Baal 后解锁，每小时轮换，怪物等级随你提升。3.3 补丁把 恐惧先驱 Herald of Terror（恐惧先驱）3 层以上的稀有及以上掉率调高，掉落重心从堆 MF 转向打 Herald。 Unlocked after killing 巴尔 Baal on that difficulty. Zones rotate hourly and monster levels scale with you. 3.3 补丁 Patch 3.3 raised the Rare-or-better drop rate on tier-3+ Heralds of Terror, shifting rewards from stacking MF toward actually fighting Heralds.

🔮 术士君临 Reign of the Warlock

2026 年资料片加入的第八职业术士 Warlock，混沌 Chaos / 邪术 Eldritch / 恶魔 Demon 三系。3.3 补丁修正 死亡印记 Sigil: Death（失败不再耗蓝、火焰技能加成生效）后成为术士主推；束缚恶魔伤害被下修，转辅助定位。 The 2026 expansion added the eighth class, the Warlock, with 混沌 Chaos / 邪术 Eldritch / 恶魔 Demon trees. 3.3 补丁 Patch 3.3 fixed 死亡印记 Sigil: Death (no mana burn on failed casts, fire-skill bonuses now apply), making it the class's top build; 束缚恶魔 Bind Demon damage was tuned down into a support role.

⚔️ 练级暗金重做 Leveling Uniques Reworked

3.3 补丁集中加强一批冷门低级暗金：放血者 Bloodletter 加跑速、战枝 The Battlebranch 需求等级 25→17、灰烬灾星 Bane Ash 改施法速度、闪蝠之形 Blinkbat's Form 跑速翻倍、守望之盾 The Ward 自带一孔，天使之袍全套额外 +1 全技能。 Patch 3.3 buffed a batch of overlooked low-level uniques: 放血者 Bloodletter gains run speed, 战枝 The Battlebranch's required level drops 25→17, 灰烬灾星 Bane Ash pivots to cast rate, 闪蝠之形 Blinkbat's Form doubles its run speed, 守望之盾 The Ward comes with a socket, and full 天使之袍 Angelic Raiment adds +1 to all skills.

🧿 赛季符文之语轮转 Seasonal 符文之语 Runeword Rotation

上一批天梯专属符文之语 Mania、Hysteria、蜕变 Metamorphosis、大地 Ground、锻造 Temper、壁炉 Hearth、治愈 Cure、壁垒 Bulwark 已在第 15 赛季开放给非天梯；本赛季另有经过平衡调整的新版天梯专属替换。 Last expansion's ladder-only runewords — Mania, Hysteria, 蜕变 Metamorphosis, 大地 Ground, 锻造 Temper, 壁炉 Hearth, 治愈 Cure and 壁垒 Bulwark — are now available in non-ladder for Season 15; this season ships rebalanced new ladder-only versions instead.

🏆 天梯第 15 赛季 Ladder Season 15

2026 年 8 月 21 日开启（3.3 补丁同步上线）。定期重置的天梯赛季，冲级、竞速、专属奖励，是新 meta 的试验场。 Opened 21 August 2026 alongside patch 3.3. 天梯 Ladder seasons reset periodically — leveling races, speed runs and ladder-only rewards make them the proving ground for every new meta.
