圣骑士 Paladin

圣骑士是「光环 + 祝福之锤」的代名词，也是开荒与终局最稳的近战/法系混合职业。祝福之锤圣骑士几乎能单人打通全剧并高效 farm，是新手与老玩家共同的首选。 The Paladin is synonymous with auras and 祝福之锤 Blessed Hammer, and he is the most reliable melee/caster hybrid for both progression and endgame. A 祝福之锤圣骑士 Hammerdin can solo the entire game and farm efficiently, which is why veterans and beginners alike pick him first.

定位： Role:魔法锤 / 近战 / 辅助 · 优势：祝福之锤全能、光环团队增益、盾击专克超级首领 Boss · 劣势：锤子需练手（螺旋弹道）、近战流吃装备。 Magic hammers / melee / support · Pros: 祝福之锤 Blessed Hammer does everything, auras buff the party, 盾击 Smite hard-counters 超级 Uber bosses · Cons: hammers take practice (spiral trajectory) and melee builds are gear hungry.

主流流派 Meta Builds

S 级 · 全能魔法 S-Tier · All-Round Magic

祝福之锤圣骑士 Hammerdin

最经典的圣骑士流派。祝福之锤造成魔法伤害，几乎无视所有免疫（仅魔法免疫怪需破免），配合 专注 Concentration 光环大幅提升伤害与命中，单人通全剧 + 高效 farm。 The most iconic Paladin build. 祝福之锤 Blessed Hammer deals magic damage that bypasses nearly every immunity (only magic immunes need a 破免 Sunder), and the 专注 Concentration aura massively boosts its damage — perfect for soloing the game and farming fast.

A 级 · 物理近战 A-Tier · Physical Melee

热诚圣骑士 Zealot

Zeal 多段攻 + 狂热 Fanaticism光环，高攻速高爆发单体与清场兼具，是经典的物理近战路线。 Zeal's multi-hit swings plus the 狂热 Fanaticism aura give huge attack speed and burst for both single targets and packs — the classic physical melee route.

S 级 · 超级 Uber 专杀 S-Tier · 超级 Uber Killer

天堂之拳/盾击圣骑士 FoH / Smiter

Fist of the Heavens（天堂之拳）魔法+电混合 AoE，配合 信念 Conviction 在混沌圣所等地极强；Smiter 用 盾击 Smite 必中打 超级 Uber。 Fist of the Heavens mixes magic and lightning AoE and, backed by 信念 Conviction, shreds places like the 混沌避难所 Chaos Sanctuary; the Smiter variant uses 盾击 Smite's guaranteed hits to kill 超级场景 Ubers.

开荒与通用技巧 Progression & General 技巧 Tips

  • 新手必练 祝福之锤圣骑士 Hammerdin： Every beginner should try a 祝福之锤圣骑士 Hammerdin:祝福之锤（魔法伤害）绕开绝大多数免疫，光环提供续航，开荒到终局通吃。 Blessed Hammer's magic damage bypasses nearly all immunities and the auras keep you going from leveling to endgame.
  • 锤子弹道： Hammer trajectory:祝福之锤沿螺旋轨迹飞行，需练习站位让锤子扫过怪群；活力 Vigor 提升移动与锤速。 Hammers spiral outward, so you must position yourself for them to sweep through the pack; 活力 Vigor speeds up both you and the hammers.
  • Smiter 打 超级 Uber： Smiters for 超级场景 Ubers:盾击（盾击 Smite）必中且无视格挡，配合 悲伤 Grief + 暗金圣盾专杀超级 boss。 Smite always hits and ignores block; with 悲伤 Grief and a unique paladin shield it deletes 超级 Uber bosses.
  • Fanaticism / 信念 Conviction： Fanaticism / 信念 Conviction:狂热给物理输出，信念降抗，是组队核心光环。 Fanaticism for physical damage, 信念 Conviction for resist reduction — the two key party auras.
  • Holy Shield： Holy Shield:大幅提升格挡与防御，近战/盾击流必点。 A huge boost to block and defence — mandatory for melee and Smiter builds.
