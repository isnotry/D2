亡灵法师 亡灵法师 Necromancer

亡灵法师是「让别人干活」的大师：召唤亡灵大军、施放毒与骨系法术、并用诅咒削弱敌人。召唤流是新手最友好的开荒流派（安全、省力），毒系与骨系则在终局拥有不俗输出。 The 亡灵法师 Necromancer is the master of letting others do the work: raise an undead army, sling poison and bone spells, and cripple enemies with curses. 召唤流 Summoner is the most beginner-friendly progression build (safe and low effort), while Poison and Bone deliver solid endgame damage.

定位： Role:召唤 / 毒 / 骨系 · 优势：召唤流极安全、毒系 AoE 强 · 劣势：召唤清 boss 慢、骨系依赖 死亡之网 Death's Web。 Summons / Poison / Bone · Pros: 召唤流 Summoner is extremely safe, Poison has great AoE · Cons: 召唤流 Summoner kills bosses slowly, Bone depends on 死亡之网 Death's Web.

技能树 Skill Trees

召唤 Summoning

亡灵大军作战，最安全的路线。 Let the undead army fight for you — the safest route.

  • 复活骷髅 Raise Skeleton
  • 骷髅掌握 Skeleton Mastery
  • 黏土石魔 Clay Golem / 钢铁石魔 Iron Golem
  • 重生 Revive
  • Summon Resist（抗） 召唤抵抗 Summon Resist (minion resistances)

毒与骨 Poison & Bone

直接伤害系，骨矛/骨魂与毒 nova。 Direct damage: 骨矛 Bone Spear/精神 Spirit plus 剧毒新星 Poison Nova.

  • 毒牙 Poison Dagger / 剧毒新星 Poison Nova
  • 骨矛 Bone Spear / 骨魂 Bone Spirit
  • 骨牢 Bone Prison / 骨墙 Bone Wall
  • 尸体爆炸 Corpse Explosion
  • 牙 Teeth

诅咒 Curses

削弱敌人，全流派通用。 削弱 Weaken enemies — useful for every build.

  • 伤害加深 Amplify Damage
  • 衰老 Decrepify
  • 微暗灵视 Dim Vision / 吸引 Attract
  • Lower Resist（毒系核心） 降低抵抗 Lower Resist (core for poison)
  • 钢铁处女 Iron Maiden / 偷取生命 Life Tap

毒系死灵 毒亡灵法师 Poison Necromancer

Poison 新星 Nova 范围毒伤，配合 降低抵抗 Lower Resist 与毒 破免 Sunder，恐惧 Terror Zone 清场效率极高，是 2.5+ 赛季强势流派。 剧毒新星 Poison Nova blankets packs in poison; combined with 降低抵抗 Lower Resist and a 破毒免 Poison Sunder it clears 恐怖地带 Terror Zones extremely fast — a strong pick since patch 2.5.

骨系死灵 骨亡灵法师 Bone Necromancer

Bone 精神 Spirit / 骨矛 Bone Spear 远程高伤，不吃免疫（魔法伤害，仅少数魔法免疫需破免）。依赖 死亡之网 Death's Web 与暗金项链堆技能。 骨魂 Bone Spirit and 骨矛 Bone Spear deal heavy ranged magic damage that almost nothing resists (only the rare magic immune needs a 破免 Sunder). It leans hard on 死亡之网 Death's Web and a +skills amulet.

开荒与通用技巧 Progression & General 技巧 Tips

  • 新手首选召唤： 召唤流 Summoner first for beginners:骷髅海 + 黏土石魔 + 重生，自己躲在后面，几乎零风险通关。 A wall of skeletons, a 黏土石魔 Clay Golem and revives let you hide in the back and clear the game with almost no risk.
  • 诅咒位核心： 诅咒 Curses are core:Amplify Damage（增伤）给物理召唤用，衰老 Decrepify减速+易伤，超级 Uber 也靠它。 伤害加深 Amplify Damage boosts your physical minions, while 衰老 Decrepify slows and softens — it is also the key curse for 超级场景 Ubers.
  • 毒系需 -% 毒抗： Poison needs -% poison resist:Lower Resist（降抗）+ 毒 破免 Sunder，配合 死亡之网 Death's Web / Trang-Oul 大幅提升。 降低抵抗 Lower Resist plus a 破毒免 Poison Sunder, backed by 死亡之网 Death's Web / 特朗奥 Trang-Oul's, multiplies your damage.
  • 骨系标杆装备： Bone's benchmark gear:Death's Web 死灵法杖 + 暗金项链，骨魂 Bone Spirit / 骨矛 Bone Spear 高伤。 死亡之网 Death's Web plus a +skills unique amulet pushes 骨魂 Bone Spirit / 骨矛 Bone Spear damage sky-high.
  • Corpse Explosion： 尸体爆炸 Corpse Explosion:尸体爆炸是强力 AoE，群怪时让召唤先杀几个再连环爆。 It is a monster AoE tool — let your minions kill a few, then chain-detonate the corpses.
