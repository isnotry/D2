符文之语图鉴 符文之语 Runewords

符文之语是将特定符文按顺序排列镶嵌到带孔装备中激活的强大词缀。下表汇集 D2R 当前版本最具代表性的符文之语，包括 2.4–2.6 赛季新增与经典终局装备。 符文之语 Runewords are powerful affixes you unlock by socketing specific runes in the right order into a socketed item. The table below collects the most representative runewords of the current D2R patch, including the season 2.4–2.6 additions and classic endgame gear.

提示： Tip:符文顺序与孔数必须完全匹配，且底材类型/品质需符合（普通/卓越/精英）。高符文（#26 Vex、#27 Ohm、#28 Lo、#30 Ber、#31 Jah、#32 Cham、#33 Zod）极其珍贵，优先用于 谜团 Enigma / 无限 Infinity / 悲伤 Grief 等核心。 The rune order and socket count must match exactly, and the base type/quality must qualify (normal/exceptional/elite). High runes (#26 Vex, #27 Ohm, #28 Lo, #30 Ber, #31 Jah, #32 Cham, #33 Zod) are extremely precious — prioritise them for core runewords like 谜团 Enigma, 无限 Infinity and 悲伤 Grief.
第 15 赛季轮转： Season 15 rotation:每个新赛季开始时会例行把上一批「天梯专属」内容开放给非天梯。本季轮到的是 Mania、Hysteria、蜕变 Metamorphosis、大地 Ground、锻造 Temper、壁炉 Hearth、治愈 Cure、壁垒 Bulwark 这八个符文之语；术士君临资料片下另有经过数值调整的新版天梯专属接管，所以新旧版本的实际词缀会有差异。开局前先确认你手上的版本属于哪一批。 At the start of each new season, the previous batch of "ladder-only" content is routinely opened up to non-ladder. This time it's the eight runewords Mania, Hysteria, 蜕变 Metamorphosis, 大地 Ground, 锻造 Temper, 壁炉 Hearth, 治愈 Cure and 壁垒 Bulwark; 术士君临 Reign of the Warlock ships numerically adjusted new ladder-only versions to take over, so the actual affixes differ between old and new. Check which batch you're holding before you commit resources.
