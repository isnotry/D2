符文之语图鉴 符文之语 Runewords

符文之语是将特定符文按顺序排列镶嵌到带孔装备中激活的强大词缀。下表汇集 D2R 当前版本最具代表性的符文之语：2.4 起的赛季新增、3.0「术士君临」的五个新符文之语、3.4 调整，以及经典终局装备。 符文之语 Runewords are powerful affixes you unlock by socketing specific runes in the right order into a socketed item. The table below collects the most representative runewords of the current D2R patch: the season additions from 2.4 onward, the five new runewords of 3.0 术士君临 Reign of the Warlock, the 3.4 changes, and classic endgame gear.

提示： Tip:符文顺序与孔数必须完全匹配，且底材类型/品质需符合（普通/卓越/精英）。高符文（#26 Vex、#27 Ohm、#28 Lo、#30 Ber、#31 Jah、#32 Cham、#33 Zod）极其珍贵，优先用于 谜团 Enigma / 无限 Infinity / 悔恨 Grief 等核心。 The rune order and socket count must match exactly, and the base type/quality must qualify (normal/exceptional/elite). High runes (#26 Vex, #27 Ohm, #28 Lo, #30 Ber, #31 Jah, #32 Cham, #33 Zod) are extremely precious — prioritise them for core runewords like 谜团 Enigma, 无限 Infinity and 悔恨 Grief.
第 15 赛季轮转（已生效）： Season 15 rotation (live):每个新赛季开始时会例行把上一批「天梯专属」内容开放给非天梯。本季轮到的是 Mania、Hysteria、变形 Metamorphosis、接地 Ground、淬火 Temper、壁炉 Hearth、解药 Cure、壁垒 Bulwark 这八个符文之语；术士君临资料片下另有经过数值调整的新版天梯专属接管，所以新旧版本的实际词缀会有差异。开局前先确认你手上的版本属于哪一批。 At the start of each new season, the previous batch of "ladder-only" content is routinely opened up to non-ladder. This time it's the eight runewords Mania, Hysteria, 变形 Metamorphosis, 接地 Ground, 淬火 Temper, 壁炉 Hearth, 解药 Cure and 壁垒 Bulwark; 术士君临 Reign of the Warlock ships numerically adjusted new ladder-only versions to take over, so the actual affixes differ between old and new. Check which batch you're holding before you commit resources.
3.4 补丁（第 16 赛季）： Patch 3.4 (Season 16):官方补丁说明没有新增符文之语，只重做了 永恒 Eternity：底材由 5 孔近战武器扩大到近战/远程武器，攻速与伤害上调，并新增按角色等级提升最大伤害（完整词缀见下表）。该改动只在术士君临线上天梯与离线生效，线上非天梯沿用旧版。同时第 15 赛季的 10 件天梯专属暗金/套装转入非天梯（清单见练级页）。 The official patch notes add no new runewords and only rebuild 永恒 Eternity: its base expands from 5-socket melee weapons to melee and missile weapons, attack speed and damage go up, and it gains max damage by character level (full affix list in the table below). These changes apply only to RotW online 天梯 Ladder and Offline; online non-ladder keeps the old version. The ten Season 15 ladder-only uniques/sets also move to non-ladder (see the 升级 Leveling guide for the list).
