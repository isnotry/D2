野蛮人 Barbarian

野蛮人是近战与「战吼」大师，拥有全游戏最强的光环类增益（战斗体制 Battle Orders 提升生命/法力上限）与独特的 寻物 Find Item双倍掉落机制。虽然开荒偏慢，但终局 旋风 Whirlwind 与 寻物狂战 Pitzerker 都是顶级 farm 流派。 The Barbarian is the master of melee and warcries. He brings the strongest party buff in the game (战斗体制 Battle Orders raises max life and mana) and the unique 寻物 Find Item mechanic that rolls loot a second time. 升级 Leveling is slow, but at endgame both 旋风 Whirlwind and the 寻物狂战 Pitzerker are top-tier farming builds.

定位： Role:近战 / 战吼辅助 · 优势：BO 团队增益、寻物 Find Item 双倍掉落、WW 清场爽快 · 劣势：开荒慢、吃装备、需要解决命中率。 Melee / warcry support · Pros: 战斗体制 Battle Orders for the party, 寻物 Find Item double drops, 旋风 Whirlwind clears feel great · Cons: slow leveling, gear hungry, needs attack rating.

A 级 · 近战清场 A-Tier · Melee Clear

旋风野蛮人 Whirlwind Barbarian

Whirlwind 边转边打，清场爽快、机动强。配合 战斗体制 Battle Orders 与 high IAS 装备，是经典的近战 farm 流派。 Whirlwind lets you spin through packs while dealing damage — fast, mobile and satisfying. With 战斗体制 Battle Orders and high IAS gear it is the classic melee farming build.

开荒与通用技巧 Progression & General 技巧 Tips

  • 开荒偏慢： Slow to level:野蛮人前期脆、命中率低，建议先练个小号或借装备；可用 战吼野蛮人 Singer（战吼）过渡。 Early Barbarians are squishy with poor attack rating, so twink some gear from another character or level as a 战吼野蛮人 Singer first.
  • Battle Orders 是灵魂： Battle Orders is everything:大幅提升全队生命/法力上限，组队必带。 It massively raises the party's max life and mana — a must-have in groups.
  • Find Item（hork）： Find Item (horking):杀死 boss 后对尸体用寻物，有几率再掉一次，MF 机核心。 After killing a boss, hork the corpse for a chance at a second drop — the heart of the MF Barb.
  • Whirlwind 吃攻速与命中： Whirlwind wants IAS and AR:用 亚瑞特之脸 Arreat's Face、Highlord's 等堆 IAS 与致命。 Stack IAS and deadly strike with pieces like 亚瑞特之脸 Arreat's Face and 大君之怒 Highlord's Wrath.
  • Pitzerker 双倍掉落： Pitzerker double drops:Berserk（魔法伤，无视免疫）+ 寻物 Find Item，彼特 The Pit 刷装备效率第一。 Berserk (magic damage, ignores physical immunity) plus 寻物 Find Item makes 彼特 The Pit the fastest gear farm in the game.
  • Patch 3.3 让野蛮人变强了： Patch 3.3 made the Barbarian stronger:放血者 Bloodletter新增 +20% 跑速且能更早拿到（物品等级 34→30、需求等级 25→17），玛那德之愈 Manald Heal新增 +10% 施法速度，两条加起来让「双持放血者旋风」第一次成为有竞争力的天梯起步路线；此外 跳跃 Leap 落地动画卡死的 bug 也已修复。往年想练野蛮人但嫌开荒太吃装备的话，本季是个好时机。 放血者 Bloodletter gains +20% run speed and drops much earlier (item level 34→30, required level 25→17), and 玛那德之愈 Manald Heal gains +10% faster cast rate — together they make dual-放血者 Bloodletter 旋风 Whirlwind a genuinely competitive 天梯 Ladder starter for the first time. The 跳跃 Leap landing-animation lock was also fixed. If you've always wanted to play a Barbarian but found the early 天梯 Ladder too gear-dependent, this is the season to try it.
