刺客 Assassin

刺客是陷阱与武学的宗师。陷阱刺客 Trapsin用闪电/死亡陷阱远程清场，是最强的 solo 通关流派之一；而 马赛克武学刺客 Mosaic 武学刺客凭借新符文之语实现了「永久蓄力」，化身全屏元素风暴，是当前版本的顶级战力。 The Assassin is the master of traps and martial arts. A 陷阱刺客 Trapsin clears screens at range with 闪电 Lightning and Death Sentries and is one of the safest solo-clearing builds; the 马赛克 Mosaic martial-arts Assassin uses the new runeword to make charges permanent, becoming a screen-wide elemental storm and a top-tier force in the current patch.

定位： Role:陷阱远程 / 武学近战 · 优势：陷阱刺客 Trapsin 极安全、马赛克 Mosaic 全屏 AoE 顶级 · 劣势：马赛克 Mosaic 双爪难凑、武学需练习蓄力节奏。 Ranged traps / melee martial arts · Pros: 陷阱刺客 Trapsin is extremely safe, 马赛克 Mosaic's screen-wide AoE is top-tier · Cons: a 马赛克 Mosaic claw pair is hard to assemble, martial arts needs practice on charge timing.

陷阱刺客 Trapsin

Lightning Sentry + 死亡守卫 Death Sentry 提供电/火/物三系混合伤害，配合 shadow 系控场，几乎无免疫盲点，是最强 solo 通关流派之一。 Lightning Sentry plus 死亡守卫 Death Sentry deal mixed lightning/fire/physical damage; with 影子训练 Shadow Disciplines for control there is almost no immunity blind spot, making 陷阱刺客 Trapsin one of the strongest solo-clearing builds.

马赛克武学刺客 Mosaic 武学 Martial Arts

双持 马赛克 Mosaic 爪实现 100% 不消耗蓄力，蓄好 凤凰击 Phoenix Strike / 雷爪 Claws of Thunder / 冰刃 Blades of Ice 后狂踩 龙爪 Dragon Talon，全屏火/电/冰齐发，当前版本顶级战力。 Dual 马赛克 Mosaic claws give a 100% chance not to consume charges; bank 凤凰击 Phoenix Strike / 雷爪 Claws of Thunder / 冰刃 Blades of Ice, then spam 龙爪 Dragon Talon to unleash screen-wide fire/lightning/cold — a top-tier build in the current patch.

开荒与通用技巧 Progression & General 技巧 Tips

  • 开荒首选 陷阱刺客 Trapsin： Trapsin for progression:Lightning Sentry + 死亡守卫 Death Sentry 三系混合伤害，几乎无免疫盲点，shadow 系控场保命。 Lightning Sentry plus 死亡守卫 Death Sentry deal mixed damage across three elements with almost no immunity gap, and the Shadow tree controls and keeps you alive.
  • Mosaic 是革命： Mosaic is a revolution:双持 马赛克 Mosaic 爪有 100% 概率不消耗蓄力，蓄好 凤凰击 Phoenix Strike 等后狂踩 龙爪 Dragon Talon 即可全屏放技能。 Dual 马赛克 Mosaic claws have a 100% chance not to consume charges, so bank 凤凰击 Phoenix Strike and spam 龙爪 Dragon Talon to unleash screen-wide skills.
  • 蓄力机制： Charge mechanic:武学刺客先蓄 charge-up 再放 finisher；马赛克 Mosaic 下每 14 秒需接触敌人刷新计时。 Martial-arts Assassins charge up before releasing a finisher; under 马赛克 Mosaic you must touch an enemy every 14s to refresh the timer.
  • Shadow 系保命： Shadow tree survival:Cloak of Shadows 致盲、心灵爆震 Mind Blast 眩晕/转化、消退 Fade 加抗减伤、武器格挡 Weapon Block 格挡。 Cloak of Shadows blinds, 心灵爆震 Mind Blast stuns/converts, 消退 Fade adds resist and damage reduction, and 武器格挡 Weapon Block gives blocking.
  • Death Sentry 尸爆： Death Sentry corpse explosion:死亡陷阱触发尸体爆炸，群怪清场利器。 Death Sentry triggers corpse explosions — a great tool for clearing packs.
