亚马逊 亚马逊 Amazon

亚马逊是远程输出的多面手，三大系分别对应 标枪/长矛、弓/十字弓 与 被动闪避。她拥有游戏里最强的范围电系输出（闪电之怒 Lightning Fury），同时被动系提供极高生存，是最适合开荒与终局 farm 的职业之一。 The 亚马逊 Amazon is the all-round ranged damage dealer. Her three trees cover Javelin/Spear, Bow/弩 Crossbow and passive evasion. She has the best AoE lightning damage in the game (闪电之怒 Lightning Fury) while her passives give huge survivability, making her one of the best classes for both progression and endgame farming.

定位： Role:远程物理/元素输出 · 优势：高机动、高生存、闪电之怒 Lightning Fury 清屏极强 · 劣势：单体 boss 与电免处理需要 无限 Infinity/破免。 Ranged physical/elemental damage · Pros: high mobility, high survivability, 闪电之怒 Lightning Fury shreds packs · Cons: single-target bosses and lightning immunes need 无限 Infinity/破免 Sunder.

技能树 Skill Trees

标枪与长矛 Javelin & Spear

以闪电之怒为核心的范围电系爆发，兼具体伤。 AoE lightning burst built around 闪电之怒 Lightning Fury, with solid single-target too.

  • Lightning 狂怒 Fury（21 级可学，核心 AoE） 闪电之怒 Lightning Fury (level 21, core AoE)
  • Charged Strike（单体电系） 充能一击 Charged Strike (single-target lightning)
  • 强力打击 Power Strike / 闪电打击 Lightning Strike
  • Poison Javelin（备选） 毒枪 Poison Javelin (optional)
  • 瘟疫标枪 Plague Javelin

弓与弩 Bow & Crossbow

物理/冰远程路线，依赖高伤弓与攻速。 Physical/cold ranged route that relies on a high-damage bow and IAS breakpoints.

  • 冰冻箭 Freezing Arrow
  • 多重箭 Multishot / 扫射 Strafe
  • 导引箭 Guided Arrow / 爆裂箭 Immolation Arrow
  • Magic Arrow（省箭） 魔法箭 Magic Arrow (saves arrows)

被动与魔法 Passive & Magic

提供闪避、暴击与雇佣兵增益，是生存核心。 Evasion, crits and 瓦尔基里 Valkyrie support — the core of her survivability.

  • Dodge / 回避 Evade / 闪避 Avoid（三闪） 躲避 Dodge / 回避 Evade / 闪避 Avoid (the three D/E/A)
  • 致命打击 Critical Strike
  • Penetration（AR） 穿透 Penetration (attack rating)
  • 瓦尔基里 Valkyrie
  • 诱饵 Decoy / 慢速箭 Slow Missiles

主流流派 Meta Builds

S 级 · 范围电系 S-Tier · AoE 闪电 Lightning

标枪亚马逊 Javelin 亚马逊 Amazon

游戏最强清屏流派之一。闪电之怒 Lightning Fury 对成群敌人造成毁灭性电伤，充能一击 Charged Strike 处理单体 boss；不吃命中率，开荒到终局通吃。 One of the strongest screen-clearing builds in the game. 闪电之怒 Lightning Fury deals devastating lightning damage to packed enemies while 充能一击 Charged Strike handles single bosses; it ignores attack rating and works from leveling all the way to endgame.

A 级 · 物理/冰远程 A-Tier · Physical/冰冷 Cold Ranged

弓亚马逊 Bow 亚马逊 Amazon

以 冰冻箭 Freezing Arrow 控场、多重箭 Multishot/扫射 Strafe 输出的物理路线，手感爽快但装备门槛较高，需要高伤弓与攻速档位支撑。 A physical route that locks packs down with 冰冻箭 Freezing Arrow and burns them with 多重箭 Multishot/扫射 Strafe. It feels great but is gear hungry — you need a high-damage bow and the right IAS breakpoints.

玩法要点 Playstyle 技巧 Tips

  • 注意武器攻速（IAS）档位 Mind your weapon IAS breakpoints
  • Freezing Arrow 控场后 多重箭 Multishot 输出 Lock packs with 冰冻箭 Freezing Arrow, then burst with 多重箭 Multishot
  • 冰免怪切 导引箭 Guided Arrow 或物理 Swap to 导引箭 Guided Arrow or pure physical against cold immunes
  • 需配合破物/破冰护符处理免疫 Use a Physical/冰破免护符 Cold Sunder Charm for immunes
  • 生存靠走位与三闪，别硬刚 Survive by kiting and 躲避 Dodge/回避 Evade/闪避 Avoid — never tank

开荒与通用技巧 Progression & General 技巧 Tips

  • Javazon 起步最稳： 标枪亚马逊 Javazon is the safest start:前期用 强力打击 Power Strike / 充能一击 Charged Strike 清场，30 级后转 闪电之怒 Lightning Fury，配合 泰坦标枪 Titans 标枪即可横扫。 Use 强力打击 Power Strike / 充能一击 Charged Strike early, switch to 闪电之怒 Lightning Fury at level 30, and with 泰坦标枪 Titan's Revenge you steamroll everything.
  • 破电免： Breaking lightning immunes:终局建议配 无限 Infinity（惩罚）雇佣兵 + 闪电 破免护符 Sunder Charm，几乎无盲点。 For endgame run an 无限 Infinity merc (信念 Conviction) plus a 电破免护符 Lightning Sunder Charm and almost nothing resists you.
  • 被动系必点： Always invest in passives:Dodge/回避 Evade/闪避 Avoid 提供巨额闪避，瓦尔基里 Valkyrie 是优秀肉盾，建议至少 1 点。 躲避 Dodge/回避 Evade/闪避 Avoid give huge evasion and the 瓦尔基里 Valkyrie is a great meat shield — at least 1 point each.
  • 标枪无 AR 需求： Javelins need no attack rating:Lightning 狂怒 Fury 与 充能一击 Charged Strike 不吃命中率，开荒体验远好于物理系。 闪电之怒 Lightning Fury and 充能一击 Charged Strike ignore AR, which makes leveling far smoother than the physical trees.
  • Bowazon 备选： 弓亚马逊 Bowazon as an alternative:Freezing Arrow / 多重箭 Multishot（或 扫射 Strafe）物理+冰，需要深造武器（风之力 Windforce / 信心 Faith）与攻速档位。 冰冻箭 Freezing Arrow with 多重箭 Multishot (or 扫射 Strafe) mixes cold and physical, but needs an endgame bow (风之力 Windforce / 信心 Faith) and proper IAS breakpoints.
