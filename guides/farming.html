速刷与 MF 指南 Farming

本页内容整理自项目内文档（MF.md / 卓古拉之握.md），聚焦通关后纯冰法速刷与暗金（卓古拉之握）速刷路线，是实战向的刷宝手册。MF（魔法寻获 Magic Find，魔法寻获）越高，亮金/套装/符文掉率越高，但会牺牲部分生存与输出，需平衡。 This page is compiled from the project docs (MF.md / 卓古拉之握 Dracul's Grasp.md), focusing on post-game pure-cold-Sorc farming and the unique (卓古拉之握 Dracul's Grasp) farming route — a practical loot guide. Higher MF (魔法寻获 Magic Find) raises rare/set/rune drop rates, but trades away some survivability and damage, so balance it.

核心思路： Core idea:练一个纯冰法师（暴风雪/冰封球）→ 力量刚好穿装备、其余全血、格挡 75%、MF 300+。先用冰免怪少的 85 场景起量，再用 无限 Infinity 佣兵破冰免，最后靠 破免 Sunder/副手换装把 MF 顶到 450–550。3.3 补丁提醒：破免在非地狱难度不再随 MF 掉落，且 Herald 掉率完全不吃 MF，本季要把清场速度与 Herald 层数排在 MF 数字前面。 Level a pure cold Sorc (暴风雪 Blizzard/冰封球 Frozen Orb) → just enough Strength for gear, rest Vitality, 75% block, MF 300+. Start with 85 areas that have few cold immunes to build volume, then break cold immunes with an 无限 Infinity mercenary, and finally push MF to 450–550 via 破免 Sunder/swap gear. 3.3 补丁 Patch 3.3 caveat: Sunders no longer drop from 魔法寻获 Magic Find outside Hell and Herald rates ignore MF entirely, so this season prioritise clear speed and Herald tiers over raw MF numbers.

四、刷宝循环示例 4 · Sample 速刷 Farming Loop

  1. 开局城镇 → 传送 远古通道 → 暴风雪清图 → 捡亮金/套装/符文 → 回城 Start in town → teleport to the 远古通道 Ancient Tunnels → 暴风雪 Blizzard the map → grab rares/sets/runes → return to town
  2. 换场：地穴 → 暴躁外皮 → 墨菲斯托 Switch maps: the 深坑 Pits → Pindleskin → 墨菲斯托 Mephisto
  3. 每 15–20 分钟一轮，效率约 50–80 场/小时 One loop every 15–20 minutes, roughly 50–80 runs per hour

五、暗金速刷：卓古拉之握 Dracul's Grasp 5 · Unique 速刷 Farming: 卓古拉之握 Dracul's Grasp

“卓古拉之握”属精英级暗金吸血鬼骸骨手套，无固定唯一掉源，任何地狱难度怪物/首领 Boss/箱子都有可能掉。下面是一份效率高、相对省时的路线（来自项目文档）。 Dracul's Grasp is an elite unique vampire bone gloves with no fixed unique source — any Hell monster/首领 Boss/chest can drop it. Below is an efficient, relatively quick route (from the project docs).

目标 Target场景 Spot推荐原因 Why
地狱 墨菲斯托 Hell 墨菲斯托 Mephisto场景 78 Area 78路程短、传统速刷主力（3.3 补丁后卡角已封，需正面打）；日用品掉率池大，卓古拉属“常出”档；300+ MF 即可 Short path and the traditional farming mainstay (patch 3.3 closed the corner-trap, fight head-on); large rare pool, Dracul's is 'common'; 300+ MF is enough
地狱 安达利尔（恐怖地带） Hell 安达利尔 Andariel (Terror Zone)≥85 时 At ≥85恐怖地带刷新后掉落池升级到 85，精英暗金掉率更高；比劳模更省路程 A Terror Zone upgrades the drop pool to 85, higher elite-unique rate; less walking than Meph
地狱 迪亚波罗（混沌避难所） Hell 迪亚波罗 Diablo (混沌避难所 Chaos Sanctuary)8585 场景=最高掉落池，所有精英暗金都能掉；清场慢但顺带刷符文 An 85 area = top drop pool, every elite unique can drop; slow clear but farms runes too
备选 85 场景： Alternative 85 areas:古代通道（冰法最爱）、世界之石大殿 1–3 层 + 巴尔王座、奶牛关——这些 85 场景所有精英暗金都能掉，可顺带刷符文/底材。 The 远古通道 Ancient Tunnels (cold Sorc's favourite), the Worldstone Keep 1–3 + Throne of Destruction, and the Secret Cow Level — all 85 areas can drop every elite unique, and farm runes/bases on the side.

懒人速刷方案 Lazy 速刷 Farming Plan

  1. 练个冰法（暴风雪/冰封球）→ 力量够穿装备，其余全血，格挡 75%，MF 300+ Level a cold Sorc (暴风雪 Blizzard/冰封球 Frozen Orb) → enough Strength for gear, rest Vitality, 75% block, MF 300+
  2. 每天上线 10 分钟：先看恐怖地带是否刷在 A1/A2（恐怖安姐 2 分钟一把）；不在则 劳模（卡角已封，约 1 分钟一把），刷 20 轮收工 10 minutes a day: check if a Terror Zone is in A1/A2 (Terror 安达利尔 Andariel ~2 min a run); if not, run 墨菲斯托 Mephisto (~1 min a run now that the corner-trap is gone), 20 runs and done
  3. 周末有空：去古代通道或混沌避难所深度刷，顺带刷符文/底材 On weekends: farm the 远古通道 Ancient Tunnels or 混沌避难所 Chaos Sanctuary in depth, also grabbing runes/bases
掉率真相： Drop-rate truth:卓古拉之握掉率约 1:几千～1:几万（跟军帽、乌鸦同量级），属“日用品”但不算常见；劳模/安姐/85 场景轮着刷，平均 1–3 天可见一双（300+ MF 前提下）。 Dracul's Grasp drops around 1:several-thousand to 1:several-tens-of-thousand (same tier as Harlequin Crest or 霜之乌鸦 Raven Frost) — a 'common' unique but not frequent; rotate Meph/安达利尔 Andariel/85-areas and you'll see a pair every 1–3 days (at 300+ MF).

实在刷不到的替代 Alternatives If You Can't Drop It

  • 聚气“偷取生命”杖 Charge staff of '偷取生命 Life Tap'：普通难度 A2 卓格南商店刷一根“生命偷取”聚气杖，进场先手动放诅咒再换盾击 In Normal A2, farm a '偷取生命 Life Tap' charge staff from Drognan's shop; cast the curse manually on entry, then swap to 盾击 Smite
  • 合成 CB 手套 Craft CB gloves：Blood 手套配方：4 号 + 完美红宝石 + 任意珠宝 + 重手套，可出 10% CB + 吸血/力量，成本极低、CB 值高 Blood glove recipe: #11 Amn (#4) + perfect ruby + any jewel + heavy gloves can roll 10% CB + life steal/strength at very low cost with high CB value
