术士 术士 Warlock

术士是《暗黑破坏神 II：复活》「术士君临 Reign of the Warlock」资料片在 2026 年新增的第八个职业，也是原版发售 25 年来首次加入的新职业。他是一位钻研禁忌恶魔之术的黑暗学者，通过束缚、奴役甚至吞噬恶魔来获取力量。三大技能树分别为 混沌 Chaos、邪术 Eldritch 与 恶魔 Demon。 The 术士 Warlock is the eighth class, added to 迪亚波罗 Diablo II: 重制版 Resurrected by the 2026 术士君临 Reign of the Warlock expansion — the first new class in the 25 years since the original release. He is a dark scholar of forbidden demonic arts who gains power by binding, enslaving and even devouring demons. His three skill trees are 混沌 Chaos, 邪术 Eldritch and 恶魔 Demon.

定位： Role:恶魔召唤 / 邪术近战 / 混沌远程 · 优势：独有双持机制、恶魔奴役玩法、三系皆强 · 劣势：核心伤害仅魔法+火焰（无冰/电/毒），部分免疫需针对性处理。 恶魔 Demon summoner / 邪术 Eldritch melee / 混沌 Chaos ranged · Pros: a unique two-handed-plus-offhand mechanic, demon enslavement gameplay, all three trees are strong · Cons: his core damage is only magic and fire (no cold/lightning/poison), so some immunes need special handling.

开荒与通用技巧 Progression & General 技巧 Tips

  • 2026 新职业： New class in 2026:术士来自「术士君临 Reign of the Warlock」资料片，是 D2R 时隔 25 年首个新职业，第八位登场角色。 The 术士 Warlock arrives with the 术士君临 Reign of the Warlock expansion — D2R's first new class in 25 years and the eighth to join the roster.
  • 三系定位： The three trees:Chaos（混沌）远程 AoE、邪术 Eldritch近战咒武、恶魔 Demon奴役召唤，构筑灵活。 混沌 Chaos for ranged AoE, 邪术 Eldritch for hexed melee weapons, 恶魔 Demon for enslaved summons — very flexible build options.
  • 独有机制（已修正）： Unique mechanic (since patch 3.2):Weapon Levitation 让术士可单手装备双手武器并同时持副手，但副手必须是 魔典 Grimoire才能触发——此前的「双手武器 + 精神盾」组合已被移除，配装时按魔典路线走。 武器浮空 Weapon Levitation lets the 术士 Warlock wield a two-handed weapon in one hand while keeping an offhand — but the offhand must be a 魔典 Grimoire to enable it. The old "two-hander + 精神 Spirit shield" combination has been removed, so build around a 魔典 Grimoire offhand.
  • 恶魔玩法： 恶魔 Demon gameplay:30 级解锁 绑定单体恶魔 Bind Single Demon，可绑定一只恶魔取其专属技能；吞噬 Consume 恶魔 Demon 吞噬恶魔换临时增益。 绑定单体恶魔 Bind Single Demon unlocks at level 30 and lets you borrow a demon's signature skill, while 吞噬 Consume 恶魔 Demon devours one for a temporary buff.
