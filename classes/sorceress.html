法师 Sorceress

法师是法系的标杆，唯一拥有 传送 Teleport 的职业，刷图效率与机动性无人能及。三系（冰/电/火）各有强力 build，是开荒、MF 与 boss rush 的核心。她也是新手最推荐的起手职业之一。 The Sorceress is the benchmark caster and the only class with 传送 Teleport, giving her unmatched farming speed and mobility. All three trees (冰冷 Cold/闪电 Lightning/火焰 Fire) have strong builds, making her the go-to for progression, MF and boss rushes — and one of the best starter classes for new players.

定位： Role:远程法系爆发 + 传送机动 · 优势：最快刷图、三系皆强、MF 之王 · 劣势：脆皮、遇到对应免疫需副手/佣兵。 Ranged caster burst with 传送 Teleport mobility · Pros: fastest farmer, all three trees are strong, king of MF · Cons: squishy, needs a weapon swap or merc against matching immunes.

技能树 Skill Trees

冰冷 Cold

控制与范围兼备，暴风雪是经典开荒王。 Control plus AoE — 暴风雪 Blizzard is the classic progression king.

  • 冰封球 Frozen Orb
  • 暴风雪 Blizzard
  • Ice Blast / 冰尖柱 Glacial Spike（协同） Ice Blast / 冰尖柱 Glacial Spike (synergies)
  • 碎冰甲 Frozen Armor
  • 冰冷支配 Cold Mastery

闪电 Lightning

单体与 AoE 爆发最高，但吃 -% 电抗。 Highest single-target and AoE burst, but needs -% enemy lightning resist.

  • 闪电 Lightning
  • 闪电链 Chain Lightning
  • 新星 Nova
  • Static Field（削血） Static Field (percent life burn)
  • 闪电支配 Lightning Mastery
  • 心灵传动 Telekinesis / 传送 Teleport

火焰 Fire

火球主力，火冰双修常见。 Fire Ball carries; the Meteorb hybrid is very common.

  • 火球 Fire Ball
  • 陨石 Meteor
  • 九头蛇 Hydra
  • Enchant（给佣兵/队友加火伤） Enchant (fire damage for your merc/party)
  • Warmth（回蓝） Warmth (mana regen)
  • 火焰支配 Fire Mastery

暴风雪法师 Blizzard Sorceress

经典开荒与 MF 之王。暴雪大范围高伤并减速，配合传送风筝海量怪物，拿到 破冰免 Cold Sunder 后连冰免也能打。 The classic progression and MF queen. 暴风雪 Blizzard hits a huge area for big damage and chills everything; teleport-kite whole packs, and once you own a 破冰免 Cold Sunder even cold immunes go down.

玩法要点 Playstyle 技巧 Tips

  • 传送卡位把怪聚到暴雪范围 Teleport to herd monsters into the 暴风雪 Blizzard area
  • 先 静态力场 Static Field 削血再暴雪收尾 Open with 静态力场 Static Field, then finish with 暴风雪 Blizzard

  • 堆 FCR 档位（117/200 等）提速 Hit the FCR breakpoints (117/200) for casting speed
  • Cold Mastery 减抗是伤害核心 Cold Mastery's resist reduction is the core of your damage

闪电/新星法师 Lightning / 新星 Nova Sorceress

全游戏最高 DPS 路线之一，但严重依赖 -% 敌方电抗（无限 Infinity）。闪电 Lightning 打单体、新星 Nova 清屏，终局 farm 效率顶级。 One of the highest DPS builds in the game, but heavily dependent on -% enemy lightning resist (无限 Infinity). 闪电 Lightning kills single targets, 新星 Nova clears screens — top-tier endgame farming speed.

A 级 · 双系开荒 A-Tier · Dual-Element Progression

火冰双修法师 Meteorb Sorceress

Fire Ball + 冰封球 Frozen Orb 双修，单系免疫可切换另一系，单机/开荒最稳，无需破免也能通全剧。 Fire Ball plus 冰封球 Frozen Orb: whenever one element is immune you just switch to the other. The safest solo/progression pick — it clears the whole game without any 破免护符 Sunder Charm.

玩法要点 Playstyle 技巧 Tips

  • 冰免切 火球 Fire Ball，火免切 冰封球 Frozen Orb Cold immune? Use 火球 Fire Ball. 火焰 Fire immune? Use 冰封球 Frozen Orb.
  • 开荒最稳，不依赖破免 The safest progression build — no 破免 Sunder needed
  • 传送风筝，Static 削血 Teleport-kite and soften targets with 静态力场 Static Field
  • 适合 MF 与通关 Great for MF runs and clearing the game
  • 终局可转纯冰或纯电 Respec to pure cold or pure lightning at endgame

开荒与通用技巧 Progression & General 技巧 Tips

  • 起手推荐 暴风雪 Blizzard： Start with 暴风雪 Blizzard:冰系起步最顺，30 级后暴雪成型，靠传送风筝，开荒到地狱中期都很强。 Cold is the smoothest opener; 暴风雪 Blizzard comes online at level 30 and teleport-kiting carries you well into Hell.
  • 破冰免： Breaking cold immunes:拿到 冰破免护符 Cold Sunder Charm 后冰法也能打冰免；或用副手 无限 Infinity（自己拿）降低火/电抗。 A 冰破免护符 Cold Sunder Charm lets a cold sorc kill cold immunes; a self-wielded 无限 Infinity also strips fire/lightning resist.
  • 闪电法刚需 无限 Infinity： Lightning needs 无限 Infinity:Lightning / 新星 Nova 靠 -% 电抗（无限 Infinity 佣兵或自己拿）才能打高，否则伤害骤降。 Lightning and 新星 Nova only hit hard with -% enemy lightning resist from 无限 Infinity (merc or self-wielded); without it damage collapses.
  • Teleport 是灵魂： Teleport is everything:全场景跳点、躲怪、卡位，MF 效率的关键。 Jump across zones, dodge packs and position monsters — it is the key to MF efficiency.
  • Meteorb 双修： The Meteorb hybrid:Fire Ball + 冰封球 Frozen Orb，单系免疫也能切换，单机/开荒友好。 Fire Ball plus 冰封球 Frozen Orb covers single-element immunes and is very solo/progression friendly.
